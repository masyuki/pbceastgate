About PBC Eastgate

Welcome to PBC Eastgate

A community committed to knowing Christ, growing together and making Christ known.

Who We Are

PBC Eastgate is a local church community committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, serving one another and reaching our community with the message of Jesus Christ.

We desire to provide a welcoming environment where people can encounter God, build meaningful relationships and grow spiritually.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centred community that transforms lives and impacts the community for the glory of God.

Our Mission

To glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ, nurturing believers and serving our community.

Our Values

  • Christ-centred living
  • Biblical teaching
  • Prayer
  • Fellowship
  • Service
  • Evangelism
